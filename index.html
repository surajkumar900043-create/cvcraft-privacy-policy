Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

CVCraft - Resume Builder and PDF ("CVCraft", "we", "our", or "the App") is a resume-building application designed to help users create and manage resumes on their device.

1. Information You Enter

CVCraft allows you to voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Name
  • Father's name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Address
  • Career objective
  • Education details
  • Work experience
  • Skills
  • Languages
  • Nationality
  • Hobbies
  • Profile photo

2. How Your Information Is Stored

CVCraft is designed to store your resume information locally on your device using the App's local storage mechanisms.

Your resume information and selected profile photo are not intentionally uploaded to our servers by the current functionality of the App.

3. Data Sharing

CVCraft does not currently intentionally sell, rent, or share your resume information or profile photo with third parties.

The App does not currently use advertising SDKs or analytics services to collect your resume information.

4. Internet Permission

The App requests the Android INTERNET permission.

This permission may be required for certain Android or WebView functionality. The current App functionality does not intentionally upload your resume information or profile photo to a server.

5. Profile Photo

If you choose to add a profile photo, the App processes the selected image on your device for use in your resume.

The photo is not intentionally uploaded to our servers by the current functionality of the App.

6. Data Deletion

You can remove saved resume information using the deletion or clear functionality provided within the App.

You may also remove the App and its associated local application data through Android's normal app-management features.

7. Children's Privacy

CVCraft is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children through a remote server.

8. Security

Because resume information is stored locally on your device, you should keep your device secure and avoid sharing your device or exported resume files with people you do not trust.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date will be updated.
